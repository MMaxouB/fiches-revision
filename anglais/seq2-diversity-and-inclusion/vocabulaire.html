Anglais · vocabulaire de la Cour suprême

Les six tableaux « Inside the Supreme Court » : chaque mot avec sa définition.

Key figures

† Appariement établi d'après le sens : dans les pages de cours, la colonne des lettres de ce tableau contient des chiffres.

WordDefinition
attorney / solicitorA legal professional who advises or represents clients.
chief justiceThe judge who leads a supreme court.
defendantA person accused or sued in a legal case.
judgeA person who makes decisions in a court of law.
justiceA judge serving on a supreme court.
lawyerA professional qualified to give legal advice.
plaintiff (plaignant)The person who brings a civil case to court.

Core values

WordDefinition
civil rightsRights that protect people from unfair treatment.
equalityThe principle that everyone has the same rights and status.
equityFair treatment that takes different needs into account.
fairnessThe quality of being reasonable and treating people justly.
justiceThe principle of what is right and fair under the law.
libertiesFreedoms protected from unnecessary restrictions.
responsibilityThe duty to act properly and accept consequences.

Justices and opinions

WordDefinition
adversarialBased on opposing sides arguing against each other.
collegialCharacterised by cooperation among colleagues.
conservativeFavouring traditional values or limited change.
essentialAbsolutely necessary or very important.
exemplarySo good that it can serve as a model.
impartialNot favouring either side in a dispute.
liberalGenerally supporting social reform and individual freedoms.

Court actions

WordDefinition
approve ofTo officially accept or agree to something.
challengeTo question or contest the validity of something.
dissentTo formally disagree with a majority decision.
file a lawsuitTo begin a legal case against someone.
pleadTo make a formal statement or argument in court.
protectTo keep someone or something safe from harm.

Decisions and outcomes

WordDefinition
decision / rulingAn official judgement made by a court.
enforceTo make sure a law or decision is obeyed.
overturnTo reverse a previous legal decision.
take effectTo begin to have legal force or influence.
upholdTo confirm that an earlier decision or law remains valid.

Context and impact

WordDefinition
debateA discussion in which different views are presented.
diversityThe presence of people with different backgrounds or identities.
future implicationsPossible consequences that may appear later.
implyTo suggest something without stating it directly.
landmark caseAn important legal case that sets a significant precedent.
representationThe act of speaking or acting on behalf of others.
societal changeA major shift in how society behaves or is organised.
